Nabung Woi v2 logo concepts

Each card shows the master at 256px, then 64 / 32 / 16px on light and dark, plus the Android/PWA maskable safe circle. Open this file in a browser.

A. Coin Jar

A jar with a slot, a coin about to drop in, and an up-arrow label. Reads as "saving" even at 16px because the silhouette is a lid + box + dot.

B. Piggy

The universal piggy bank, reduced to circles so it stays clean. Friendly and instantly "money", but at 16px it becomes a cream blob with a gold dot.

C. Coin Stacks

Three rising stacks of coins with a growth arrow. Says "finance / progress" more than "saving"; reads as a bar chart at small sizes.
